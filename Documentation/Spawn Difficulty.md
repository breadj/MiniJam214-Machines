DR = Difficulty Rating; SP = Spawn Point
# 'Difficulty Rating' Factors
## Single Spawns = Low DR
- Next spawn is at the same SP = low impact on DR
- Next spawn is at a close SP = medium impact on DR
- Next spawn is at a far away SP = high impact on DR
## Multiple (Simultaneous) Spawns = Medium DR
- *(should probably keep simultaneous spawns from the same point limited to 2 maximum)*
- All spawns at same SP = low impact on DR
- SPs close by each other (small area) = medium impact on DR
- SPs span entire room = high impact on DR

Each extra robot being spawned at the same has a high impact on DR.
## Spawn Point Location
- Wall SPs = low impact on DR
- Corner 2x2 grates = medium impact on DR
- Middle of room grate = high impact on DR
